## Coinbase OPFault Audit

In **August 2025** Alexis Williams from [Coinbase](https://coinbase.com) provided an impromptu audit of code related to [ENSIP-19](https://docs.ens.domains/ensip/21) primary resolution for [Base](https://base.org/).

Commit hash: [`128a7c6`](https://github.com/unruggable-labs/unruggable-gateways/tree/128a7c6d36f33b51f3c746eddfc76fdd676b1ae3)

A report was produced outlining his findings. It can be viewed [here](https://github.com/unruggable-labs/unruggable-gateways/blob/48bd9021420683c17d0350d580959191111f4188/audits/2025-08-18_Coinbase_OPFault.pdf). Mitigations were completed in the following [pull request](https://github.com/unruggable-labs/unruggable-gateways/pull/38).
